Why make a cone?

Cones and tapering shapes are structural essentials in amigurumi design. By choosing how your tip starts and expanding outward, cones allow you to build precise, rigid angles for characters, clothing, and botanical models.

Classic & Primary Uses

Amigurumi Anatomy & Features:

  • Snouts & Muzzles: Fox, cat, dog, or bear snouts that taper gracefully from a flat face to a small nose.
  • Horns & Antlers: Dragon horns, ram horns, unicorn horns, or insect antennae.
  • Tails & Spikes: Dinosaur tail tips, dragon back spikes, or cat tails.

Clothing & Accessories:

  • Wizard hats, party hats, elf hats, or witch hats.
  • Carrot bodies for snowmen or garden themes.

Botanical & Nature Elements

  • Pine trees, carrots, flower petals, or pointy leaf structures.

What are Yarn Weights?

US System uses numbers (0-7) and descriptive terms. Standard amigurumi usually relies on DK or Worsted weight yarn paired with a smaller hook to keep stitches tight.

Amigurumi Yarn & Hook Guide

Drop 1 to 3 hook sizes smaller than your yarn label recommends to keep the fabric dense and prevent stuffing from showing through.

Stuffing Types Guide

Standard polyester fiberfill (Poly-Fil) works best for cones, ensuring smooth curves without bulging.
